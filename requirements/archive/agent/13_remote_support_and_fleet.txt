TASK 13 - REMOTE SUPPORT AND FLEET
===================================
dVRK focus: the support team that keeps several sites running without seeing their research.

As built
--------
Status: BUILT and verified. Results: verification/REPORT.md, section T13.
Code: vxagent/fleet/redact.py, outbox.py, sync.py, store.py, grants.py, tools.py, desk.py;
      sites/site_b.json; vxagent/profiles.py (SUPPORT); vxagent/web/app.py. Tests: tests/test_fleet.py.
Differences from the plan below:
- Redaction is a closed description of what a record may contain (known keys, numbers, and
  strings from fixed vocabularies), checked at the outbox. A record that does not fit is refused
  and audited; nothing is cleaned. A kind outside the vocabulary is reported as "other".
- The fleet store is reached over HTTP with one token per site. It accepts a record only if its
  number is the next one for that site, so duplicates are ignored and nothing is stored out of
  order.
- The fleet desk and the edge nodes run in one process in this demonstration; a granted ticket
  is read by calling the edge node's own check directly. Between two labs that call would be an
  HTTP request to the node.
- Site B has no recordings: the ROSMA recordings are given to site A.
- The fleet view shows totals and the latest health of each site. The records are stored with
  their times, but no chart over time is drawn.
Further limits: as stated below, counts can still say how often and when a lab works.
(end of as built)

Job
---
Let a support engineer outside the lab see whether each site's agents and robot node are
healthy, what they cost and where they fail, across sites, and help with one ticket when the
site asks, without the lab's recordings, images or unpublished results ever leaving the lab
unless the lab says so for that ticket and that period.

Requirements
------------
R1  Two sites. The demonstration runs two edge nodes with different published arm files: a
    Classic PSM (PSM_KIN_SIMULATED_LARGE_NEEDLE_DRIVER_400006.json) and an Si PSM
    (PSM_Si_KIN_SIMULATED_LARGE_NEEDLE_DRIVER_420006.json).
R2  Outbox. Each edge node writes operational records to a local outbox: run counts, model
    calls, tokens, latency percentiles, error kinds, tool error rates, safety rejections by
    rule, pre-flight failures by check, node health. Records carry counts, kinds and timings.
    They carry no task text, no answers, no tool inputs or outputs, no poses and no file names.
R3  Store and forward. The outbox is sent to the fleet store when a connection exists. A node
    that is offline keeps working and keeps its outbox; on reconnection records arrive once,
    in order, without duplicates.
R4  Fleet view: sites, last contact, health, the figures of R2 over time, and sites whose
    software or configuration version differs from the others.
R5  Redaction is done at the edge, before the outbox, and tested by scanning outbox records for
    payload fields.
R6  Grant. To see the evidence of one ticket, support needs a grant from that site's safety
    lead: one ticket, read-only, with an end time. The grant is checked at the edge node on
    every call. It expires on its own, can be revoked, and every use is audited at the site.
R7  Even with a grant, support cannot move the arm, propose or approve a change, or read
    anything outside the ticket.
R8  Support assistant. Tools: fleet_overview, site_health, ops_run_stats, ops_recent_failures,
    version_drift, search of documentation and support threads, read_granted_ticket, calculator,
    remember. It answers "which sites reject most at the workspace limit" from counts, and
    "what do the dVRK threads say about this error kind" from retrieval.
R9  Node health without data access: model server reachable and models installed, retrieval
    index present, robot backend reachable, perception source latency, disk, pending approvals,
    outbox backlog.
R10 Audit at both ends: the site records who looked at what; the fleet store records who asked.

Known limits
------------
Two nodes on one machine stand in for two labs. The fleet store is one SQLite file. Counts can
still leak something about a lab's activity (how often it runs, when); that is accepted and
stated to the site.
