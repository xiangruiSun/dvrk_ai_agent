TASK 09 - TRIAL PIPELINE
=========================
dVRK focus: taking a simulation-trained policy to a physical trial. Uses the runtime of task 03
and the validation of task 02. The stages and limits follow the published deployment framework
(Sun, Wen, Liu and Munawar, "On Transferring Surgical Policies from Simulation to Reality:
Perception, Calibration, and Safety Control", under review).

As built
--------
Status: BUILT and verified on the twin. Results: verification/REPORT.md, section T09.
Code: vxagent/pipelines/policy_trial.py, vxagent/robot/preflight.py. Tests: tests/test_policy_trial.py.
Differences from the plan below:
- Reach replays real SurgicAI expert rollouts: each recorded action goes through the adapter
  generated from the policy contract (task 10) and through the safety filter. Every Approach
  demonstration starts from one pose and goes to one goal, so a trial first brings the arm to
  that start, anchored on the site's grasp pose.
- The jaw component of the recorded actions is not used: the jaw belongs to the grasp procedure.
- Place uses the published approach heights over the site's place pose; the Place
  demonstrations are not replayed.
- Staged activation: the first control step of each stage is sent alone and its tracking is
  checked before the rest of the stage.
- The needle is the declared needle of task 05. "recorded" outcomes do not exist here.
- The verdict record, the operator's confirmation and an override with its reason are kept side
  by side in the trial record.
- The twin's kinematics are checked against ROSMA: forward kinematics of the recorded joint
  values against the recorded positions, after one rigid transform per recording.
Further limits: perception is a callable with a declared latency until task 14 gives it a process.
(end of as built)

Job
---
Take a requested trial from a run sheet to a confirmed record: check that the site is ready,
rehearse on the twin, get the operator's go, run Reach, Grasp and Place under the safety filter,
compute a verdict from measurements, and stop for the operator to confirm it.

Requirements
------------
R1  Run sheet. A validated RunSheet (task 02) names the site, arm, instrument, start pose,
    envelope profile, stage sequence and acceptance criteria. Nothing runs without one.
R2  Pre-flight, all in code (vxagent/robot/preflight.py). The trial may start only when:
      - the arm is powered, enabled and homed
      - the installed instrument matches the run sheet and has an instrument file
      - a camera-to-robot calibration record exists, is well formed, has not been ended by an
        ECM move, a setup-joint move or an instrument change, and its residual is under the
        site's limit
      - the pad-plane and site records exist and are of the same session as the calibration
      - the policy contract is compatible with the site (task 10)
      - the start pose and the target lie inside the policy's training range and the workspace
      - the perception source answered within its time limit (task 14)
    Each failed check is returned with the runbook that repairs it.
R3  Rehearsal. The full stage sequence is run on the twin through the same safety filter. Any
    rejection ends the pipeline before the operator is asked.
R4  Staged activation. The operator approves the run (task 03 R10). The first motion of each
    stage is a single bounded step followed by a tracking check before the stage continues.
R5  Safety filter (vxagent/robot/safety.py), applied to every Cartesian target at the control
    rate of 10 Hz, with the published limits as the default profile:
      - step size: at most 5 mm and 5 degrees in free space; at most 2 mm and 3 degrees within
        20 mm of the needle or the phantom; smaller steps in hover, descent, lift and fine
        alignment, set per stage in the profile
      - a local workspace of +/- 40 mm
      - joint-limit margins, from the kinematic and instrument files
      - clearance of the instrument above the supporting surface, from the pad-plane record
    A target that fails any rule is rejected without clipping. After a command, the requested
    target is compared with the controller setpoint (to detect clipping by inverse kinematics)
    and with the measured pose; a difference of more than 2 mm or 2 degrees stops execution.
    A site may tighten the profile. Loosening it is a configuration change (task 02, task 12).
R6  Reach. At most 200 policy actions. Success when the measured pose is within 5 mm and
    15 degrees of the approach goal.
R7  Grasp is a geometric procedure, not a learned policy: align the jaws to the needle, bounded
    descent, close, lift, hold for two seconds.
R8  Retention. Grasp succeeds only if the needle is observed to have moved with the jaws after
    the lift and is still held after the hold. On a real system this comes from perception. On
    the twin there is no needle, so the outcome comes from an outcome source declared in the run
    sheet and written into the record: "recorded" or "injected". Not held: stop, recover.
R9  Place. Motions toward the entry site, with the needle pose carried from the last reliable
    needle-to-gripper relation while the needle is occluded. Approach heights above the entry
    are taken from the profile (the published run uses 15, 8 and 3.5 mm).
R10 Machine verdict, in code: reach tolerance met; needle held; needle tip height above and
    lateral distance from the entry; number of rejections and stops; whether recovery ran.
R11 Operator confirmation. The run pauses; the operator confirms or overrides the verdict with a
    reason. An override is kept beside the machine verdict, never in place of it.
R12 Trial record. A structured record of everything above. One model call writes a short summary
    from the record; it must pass the grounding rules of task 01 against the record, otherwise a
    template summary is used and marked as such.

Simplifications, stated
-----------------------
The twin integrates commanded poses with joint limits. It has no contact, no tissue, no needle,
no camera and no tracking error unless one is injected. The Reach and Place policies are
represented by recorded expert rollouts from the SurgicAI demonstrations, mapped through the
policy contract into the site frame; loading the trained policies themselves is an optional
backend. Needle poses come from the recorded ground truth of the AMBF scenes. Insertion, release
and thread handling are outside the pipeline, as they are outside the published evaluation.

Verification on real data (planned)
-----------------------------------
python scripts/verify.py t09    ->  verification/t09_trial_pipeline.json
  a. Safety filter boundary table: a case on each side of every limit in R5, including the
     near-contact switch at 20 mm and the tracking stop. All must match.
  b. Kinematic twin against the source: forward kinematics of the twin on recorded joint
     positions from ROSMA compared with the Cartesian positions recorded in the same samples;
     error statistics as measured. This needs both signals in the recordings; if they hold
     only one, the check is reported as not possible and the twin is checked against the
     kinematic file's own reference values instead.
  c. Pre-flight matrix: one run per failed check of R2; the pipeline must stop before any
     motion and name the repairing runbook.
  d. Trials end to end on the twin with held and not-held outcomes: path, rejections, verdict.
python scripts/verify.py t03    resume, watchdog and stale-approval cases (see task 03)

Known limits
------------
Nothing here measures task success on a robot. The published success rates belong to the paper
and its physical trials, and are not reproduced, re-measured or claimed by this pipeline.
