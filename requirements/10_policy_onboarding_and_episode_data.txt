TASK 10 - POLICY ONBOARDING AND EPISODE DATA
=============================================
dVRK focus: policy interfaces and the data policies are trained and judged on.

Job
---
A policy arrives from simulation with assumptions built in: what its observation vector means,
in which frame and unit, how its actions are scaled, how fast it expects to run, and what range
of poses it was trained on. Before it goes near a trial, write those assumptions down as a
contract, check the contract against the site, and prove that the episodes used to train or
judge it are clean and split without leakage.

Requirements
------------
R1  PolicyContract (vxagent/structured/schemas.py): policy name and version, checkpoint hash,
    source simulator, observation fields in order {name, frame, unit, size}, action space
    {size, range, meaning per component}, action scales, control rate, start-pose and goal
    ranges used in training, and the tasks it covers.
R2  Contract extraction. A structured model call drafts the contract from the policy's published
    description and configuration. Each field must point at the place in the source it came
    from; a field with no resolvable source is left empty and sent to a person. The model does
    not fill gaps.
R3  Compatibility check against a site, in code (vxagent/policy/compat.py):
      - every observation field can be produced from the site's frames and measurements
      - units agree after conversion; a field whose magnitude suggests the wrong unit is flagged
      - the control rate is achievable by the site's control loop
      - scaled actions stay inside the safety profile's step limits
      - coverage: the share of the site's workspace, start poses and targets that lies inside
        the training range
    The result is compatible, compatible within a stated sub-region, or not compatible, with
    the failing items.
R4  Out-of-range rule. A requested start pose or target outside the training range fails
    pre-flight (task 09 R2). The answer is to re-scope or retrain, as in the published work,
    never to run and see.
R5  Adapter. The observation mapping and action mapping for a contract are generated from the
    contract by code and round-trip tested: an episode mapped into policy space and back must
    return the original poses within tolerance.
R6  Episode schema. Recorded episodes from different sources are read into one schema with
    declared frame, unit and rate. Streams a source does not contain are absent, not filled.
R7  Episode checks (vxagent/data/quality.py): timestamps increase; sample-rate gaps; values
    inside the arm's joint limits; workspace magnitude consistent with the declared unit;
    one-step command jumps above a threshold; frozen signals; duplicate episodes by hash.
    An episode is accepted, accepted with flags, or excluded, with the reason.
R8  Split without leakage. Train and evaluation sets are split by operator or session, never by
    episode, and the split is checked: no operator or session on both sides.
R9  Dataset card, generated: source, licence, counts by task, excluded episodes by reason,
    split, and what the data cannot show.
R10 Authorization. These tools need the dataset scope. Remote support has no access to a site's
    episodes without a grant (task 13).

Planned code
------------
vxagent/policy/contract.py, compat.py, adapter.py
vxagent/data/episodes.py, quality.py, split.py, card.py

Real data
---------
The SurgicAI demonstrations and their published description for the contract and the adapter.
The ROSMA kinematics files, whose names carry an operator prefix, for the checks and the split.
The Classic and the Si PSM arm files published for simulated arms, for the compatibility check.

Verification on real data (planned)
-----------------------------------
python scripts/verify.py t10    ->  verification/t10_policy_and_data.json
  a. Contract extraction: fields filled with a resolvable source, fields left for a person,
     attempts; compared with a hand-written reference contract committed beside it.
  b. Compatibility of that contract with each of the two sites, and with the same site after the
     workspace is moved so that part of it leaves the training range.
  c. Adapter round trip on every demonstration: maximum position and rotation error.
  d. Episode checks over both sources: accepted, flagged, excluded, by reason.
  e. Negative controls: one episode rescaled by 1000, one with a planted jump, one duplicated,
     one with shuffled timestamps. Each must be caught by the named check.
  f. The split: operators and sessions per side, overlap (must be 0).

Known limits
------------
A contract describes an interface, not behaviour: a compatible policy can still fail. Coverage
is computed on position and a single rotation angle, not on the full pose distribution. The
unit check is a magnitude heuristic.
